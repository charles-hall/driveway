Cottage Driveway privacy policy

Updated Oct. 3, 2026

Cottage Driveway is a private app that one household uses to track vehicle maintenance, spare parts and service reminders. Only people the owner invites can sign in.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile photo. It does not get your password or access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • What you enter. Vehicle details, mileage, service history and parts inventory that you or other household members add.
  • Notification settings. If you turn on notifications, your browser gives the app a push address for that device, along with the reminder types you chose.
  • A sign-in cookie. One cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. The app doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies.

How it's used

This information is used only to run the app: to let invited people sign in, show and update the household's records, and send the reminders each person asked for. It is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for any other purpose.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a database on the app's own web server at driveway.oaksync.com. Notifications are delivered through Apple's, Google's or your browser's standard push service, which receives only the notification text.

Your choices

  • You can turn notifications on or off for each device in Settings.
  • You can sign out at any time, and you can remove the app's access from your Google account settings.
  • Ask the household owner to remove your account, and your profile, sessions and notification devices will be deleted.
